Arena Stats

Who is out there, and what they do when they get here.

What gets recorded

Every visit reports, on its own, with nothing sent by the game:

  • Country — worked out from the IP at the moment of the request; the address itself is never stored.
  • Browser and OS, screen width, language.
  • Referrer — the page someone followed a link from, which is how an unexpected visitor gets explained.

The game adds only what a pageview cannot know:

EventMeans
match-start-npA fight began, with n people holding controllers.
bot-nameOne per machine in that fight — the roster, ranked by what people actually bring.
match-end-causeHow it finished: damage, immobilized or judges.
winner-nameThe machine that won, or winner-draw.
match-length-…How long the fight lasted, bucketed.
setup-pads-nControllers plugged in at the first fight.
setup-pixel-ratio-nThe display's pixel ratio. Here because it is what hid the split-screen bug.
error-…A JavaScript error, by message, capped at five per visit.

What does not get recorded

  • No IP addresses stored.
  • No cookies, no localStorage, no tracker id — nothing is written to a visitor's browser.
  • No individual people. Everything is a count.
  • Nothing at all from localhost or file://, so local play and the probes in tools/ never reach the numbers.
  • Nothing at all when no code is configured — the script is not even loaded.
